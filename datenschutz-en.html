This website sets no cookies and embeds no external fonts, scripts, or trackers – all resources (stylesheet, images, icons) are served from the same server. That is also why you will not find a cookie banner here: there is nothing to consent to.

Pages on this website link to the Google Play Store. The link is only followed when you click it – no data is transmitted to Google beforehand, and in particular this website loads nothing from Google servers. Once you follow the link, Google processes data as its own controller, which is outside our influence; Google's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Our email mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail). Google LLC is likewise certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
