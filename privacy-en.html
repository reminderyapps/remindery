The Remindery: Birthdays App

The Short Version

Remindery: Birthdays does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or to third parties. The app works entirely without a server, without an account, without analytics or tracking services, and without advertising. Whatever you enter into the app stays on your device.

Local Data Storage

All data you enter into the app – people, birthdays, gift ideas, notes, and settings – is stored exclusively on your device. Nothing is transmitted to us or to third parties; the app does not establish any internet connection of its own.

You remain in full control: individual entries can be deleted directly in the app. Uninstalling the app removes all stored data from your device. If you create device backups (e.g., through your operating system), those backups are subject to the privacy policy of the respective backup provider.

Contacts Access (Optional)

The app offers to import names and birthdays from your address book. This access

  • happens only upon your explicit action (import feature) and after you grant the system permission,
  • is read-only – your contacts are never modified or deleted,
  • serves solely to locally import names and birthdays into the app.

Imported data never leaves your device either. You can revoke the permission at any time in your system settings; the app remains fully usable without it.

Notifications

Birthday and gift reminders are scheduled and displayed locally on your device. No third-party push services are used, and no data is transmitted for this purpose.

Data Export and Import (Optional)

The app offers an optional export of your data into a file, as well as the import of such a file. Both happen only upon your explicit action and entirely locally: the app creates the export file on your device and hands it to your operating system's share feature. Where the file ends up (e.g., your own cloud storage, an email, or another device) is entirely your decision – nothing is transmitted to us or to third parties chosen by us. The handling of the file at the destination you choose (e.g., a cloud provider) is subject to that provider's privacy policy.

No Cookies, No Tracking, No Data Sharing

The app does not use cookies or comparable technologies, does not create usage profiles, and does not embed any analytics, advertising, or social media services. No data is shared with third parties – we simply have no data that we could share.

Your Rights as a Data Subject

The GDPR grants you, among others, the rights of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), and objection (Art. 21), as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77).

Right to object under Art. 21 GDPR: Where we process data on the basis of our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) – this concerns the server logs generated when you visit this website and the handling of your email enquiries – you have the right to object to that processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will then no longer process the data unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights, and freedoms.

Regarding the app: Since the app transmits no personal data to us, these rights come to nothing there in practice – we hold no app data about which we could provide information, or which we could rectify, erase, or transfer. This does not apply to this website's server logs or to email enquiries; there you can exercise your rights without restriction. Please note for the server logs that we have no access to them ourselves – in that respect GitHub is the right addressee.
