Calendar

Days of the Week and Months

For kids ages 5 to 8 – preschool to second grade

What day is it today? How many more sleeps until my birthday? In “Days of the Week and Months” your child finds their way around the week, the year and the calendar page step by step: a sun hops from day to day, the labels fade bit by bit, and in the end your child reads a real calendar page.

What your child learns

  • The days of the week in order, yesterday, today and tomorrow up to “in five days” – on the week wheel with sleep moons
  • The four seasons and the twelve months with their numbers – on the year wheel
  • Reading the calendar page and a date such as July 3 (US) or 3 July (UK)
  • Three learning paths – read, set and match – with 76 rounds in 13 stages
  • Every task is read aloud, every word card speaks when tapped; no error sound

Made for parents

  • “Today”: the device’s real date on the week wheel, the year wheel and the calendar page
  • Your child’s birthday if you like – day and month only, on the device only, deletable at any time
  • US English with Sunday as the first day of the week, UK English with Monday, and German
  • No ads, no subscription, no account, no tracking – fully offline
  • Free: twelve rounds, then a one-time purchase
