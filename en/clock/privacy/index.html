The app

In short

The app collects nothing about your child. There is no account, no cloud and no analytics. No profiling, no ads, no nudges to share data – the app keeps everything on the device.

Children

The app does not collect any personal information from children. It has no accounts, no chat and cannot send us anything by itself. If a child emails us anyway, we use the email address only to reply once, do not use it for anything else, do not share it and delete the message promptly after replying. Parents can ask us at any time to find and delete such a message: kontakt@remindery.de.

What is stored on the device

The app only remembers what it needs to carry on: which practice rounds were completed and how well, where your child is on the learning path, which friend they picked and which settings are chosen (sound, language and voice, and whether the red and blue hands are swapped). All of this stays in the app’s storage on your device. We have no access to it. The app never asks for a name, age or date of birth.

Voice and sounds

All spoken instructions, praise and sounds are audio files inside the app – they are played, not downloaded. The app does not use the device’s text-to-speech and does not pass any text to third parties. It records nothing and does not access the microphone.

Internet, permissions and the purchase

The app itself does not connect to any server – neither ours nor anyone else’s; we don’t run any. It does not access the microphone, camera, contacts or location.

Because of the purchase, the installed app has three permissions, all of which come from Google’s Play Billing Library: internet access, checking the network status and billing through Google Play. They are needed so that the device can complete a purchase with Google Play. We use them for nothing else: there are no ads, no analytics and no sharing of data with third parties.

The version from the Amazon Appstore (for example on Fire tablets) does not have the Google Play billing permission; it keeps internet access and checking the network status, and the Amazon Appstore app on the device handles the purchase. Here too: no ads, no analytics, no sharing of data.

On iPhone and iPad, the device talks to Apple only for the purchase: the App Store handles it through the system, and the app only passes on the purchase ID. There, the feedback email and the App Store page open only after a maths question for grown-ups; the app version and the iOS version are filled in.

In the For grown-ups area, “Send a message” opens your email app and “Rate the app” opens the app’s page on Google Play (on Fire tablets, in the Amazon Appstore). The app only passes on a link; it reads nothing and sends nothing by itself. What you write goes straight from your email app to us. Only the subject line, the app version with the name of the store and the Android version are filled in.

What happens when you buy

The app is free to download; one purchase unlocks all rounds for good. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. Payment and order data are created there, and Google is responsible for them, not us.

In the app, the only trace of the purchase is a note on the device that it was bought. The app needs no account or sign-in for this: the purchase is restored from the purchase history of the Google account that is already set up on the device.

If you downloaded the app from the Amazon Appstore, you buy through Amazon. The payment and order data are then created at Amazon, and Amazon is responsible for them, not us. The purchase is restored from the purchase history of the Amazon account on the device.

On iPhone and iPad, you buy through the App Store. The payment and order data are then created at Apple, and Apple is responsible for them, not us. The purchase is restored through the Apple Account signed in on the device.

No device backup

Unlike many apps, this app is excluded from Android backup (allowBackup="false"). Your child’s progress does not go into a Google backup and does not leave the device that way either. The downside: on a new device, your child starts again from the beginning.

On iPhone and iPad, the progress is part of the device backup you set up yourself (iCloud or a computer), as with any app. That backup lives in your Apple Account or on your computer, not with us; we have no access to it.

The app store

If you downloaded the app from an app store, the store operator collects its own data about the download and installation and is responsible for it, not us. Google and Amazon give us only order data needed for accounting and tax (order number, country and, in some countries, region and postal code) – never a name or email address. Apple gives us only summed-up sales and download figures (for example per country).

Deleting data

Because everything stays on the device, you delete all of the app’s data by uninstalling it or by clearing its data in the Android settings (on iPhone and iPad: by deleting the app). We don’t need to do anything – we hold nothing.

Email from adults

This applies to emails from the app and from this website. We use your message only to answer you and delete it once your enquiry has been dealt with.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your message is about a purchase, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – our legitimate interest in answering you. Our mailbox is hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH, Gunzenhausen (Germany), under a data processing agreement.

This website

GitHub decides how long the logs are kept; for details, see GitHub’s privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/privacy

No cookies, no external resources

This website sets no cookies and embeds no external fonts, scripts or trackers – all resources are served from the same server. That is why there is no cookie banner here: there is nothing to consent to.

Links to app stores

The store badges on this website are stored on our own server. A link to Google Play or the Amazon Appstore is only followed when you click it; before that, no data goes to Google or Amazon. Once you follow a link, the respective provider processes data as its own controller, and its privacy policy applies.

Your rights and complaints

Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR, you have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20), and the right to object (Art. 21).

Right to object under Art. 21 GDPR: Where we process data on the basis of our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) – the server logs of this website and the handling of your emails – you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will then stop processing the data unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms.

In the app itself these rights have nothing to act on, because we hold no data about you or your child. They do apply to emails you send us. For the server logs, GitHub is the right addressee, as we have no access to them; for purchases, payment data and purchase history, Google or Amazon.

If you have a complaint, please contact us first at kontakt@remindery.de. We confirm receipt within 30 days and tell you what we are doing about it. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in the UK to the Information Commissioner’s Office (https://ico.org.uk), in the EU to the authority in the country where you live.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app or the operation of this website requires different processing, we will update this policy, publish it in the app and on this page, and change the date at the top. The version published here applies.
