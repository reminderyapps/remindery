Telling Time

Learn to tell time, step by step

For kids in grades 1 to 3, about ages 6 to 9 · Telling the Time in the UK and Australia · Uhr lesen lernen in German

Telling Time is a learning app for kids who are learning to read a clock with confidence, on the clock face and on the digital display. The hour hand is red, short and blunt, the minute hand blue, long and pointed – and the clock face carries both scales in the same colors: red hour numbers inside, a blue minute ring outside. A child sees at a glance which hand goes with which numbers.

This help fades away on purpose. Level by level the blue ring disappears, until the perfectly ordinary clock is left – the one in the classroom or on a watch.

Three kinds of practice, 200 rounds

  • Read the clock – the clock shows a time, the child picks the right answer
  • Set the clock – the app says a time, the child moves the hands
  • Match the clocks – clock face, digital time and time in words belong together

One step at a time: o'clock, half hours, quarter past and quarter to, 5-minute steps, then single minutes. After that come a.m. and p.m. – 8:30 a.m. or 8:30 p.m.? A sun or a moon on the clock face is the hint. About 1,200 tasks in all.

The first time the app opens, your child picks where to start: brand new to the clock, o'clock, half hours or the whole clock. Nobody repeats what they already know.

Read aloud, in your accent

Every task is read aloud, so kids who can't read yet can practice on their own. The app has American, British and Australian voices, picks the one that matches your device, and lets you switch in the For grown-ups area. All recordings are built into the app, so practice works offline: in the car, on a plane or on vacation.

What the app offers

  • Clock face with hour hand, minute hand and a minute ring, plus a digital display
  • Red and blue hands as a learning aid that fades out step by step
  • Hand colors can be swapped, to match the clock at school
  • a.m. and p.m., with a sun and a moon on the clock face as a hint
  • After a second wrong attempt, the app shows the answer instead of just marking it wrong
  • A calm learning path with growing plants instead of a points chase
  • For phones and tablets, in portrait and landscape

Made for parents

  • No ads, no subscription, no account, no tracking
  • Works without internet – only the one purchase needs a connection
  • No access to the microphone, camera, contacts or location
  • The For grown-ups area shows where your child is getting stuck
  • No time pressure, no lives, no rankings
  • The first 12 practice rounds are free, then a one-time purchase unlocks all 200 rounds for good – no subscription
