Times Tables

Times tables – understand, don’t just memorize

For grades 2 to 4

Times Tables Math Facts is multiplication practice for a few spare minutes: your child learns the times tables from easy to hard, uses dot arrays to work out the hard facts and gets every missed fact back until it sticks – with multiplication and division facts.

A fact only counts as mastered once your child answers it confidently and without hesitating on a later day. One lucky guess is not enough.

How your child learns

  • One table at a time, easy to hard: 2s, 10s and 5s, then 4s and 8s, then 3s, 6s, 9s and finally the 7s. Then everything mixed.
  • Every table in three steps: understand it with a dot array, practice, then divide (12 ÷ 3)
  • Tricks for the hard tables: 6 × 8 is 5 × 8 and one more row, 9 × 6 is 10 × 6 minus 6
  • Mistakes come back on purpose: your child’s number crossed out in red, the right one in green – in the same round, the next one and on the following days
  • Speed round: 1, 2 or 3 minutes, a best score for each length – no time pressure before that
  • No required test: start at the beginning or skip tables that already stick after five questions each
  • 16 kinds of practice, including missing numbers, turnaround facts, find the mistake and 24 read-aloud word problems

Made for parents

  • The times table chart shows every fact up to 10 × 10: new, practiced or mastered
  • Up to three children on one device, each with their own progress
  • Instructions and word problems are read aloud, in English or German
  • No ads, no subscription, no account, no tracking – fully offline
  • Free to try: the 2 and 10 times tables, a taster of the 9s and the chart, then a single one-time purchase

Available first in the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets; Google Play will follow.
