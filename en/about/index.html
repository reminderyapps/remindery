AI inside the products

Ubo – AI vocabulary trainer

Snap a page of any textbook and Claude on Amazon Bedrock turns it into structured flashcards after a short wait – any publisher, any edition, five languages. Neural pronunciation from Amazon Polly, speaking practice with on-device speech recognition: the microphone never leaves the phone. Fully serverless on AWS Lambda.

Ubo in German

Silben Lesen – learning to read

58 stories and 7 books in two-colour syllable script, 149 practice rounds and over 350 watercolour illustrations generated on a local NVIDIA RTX pipeline and curated by hand. Every word and every syllable is voiced – more than 6,000 clips.

Silben Lesen in German

Uhr lesen lernen – telling the time

200 rounds up to the 24-hour clock, with both ways Germans say the time, spoken aloud. Live in Google Play and on Amazon Fire tablets, built and shipped in about five weeks.

In development

Next up: Ubo Coach

Generic AI tutors know everything – except what is in Thursday's test. Ubo already holds the vocabulary from the child's own textbook. Ubo Coach turns it into a personal language coach that gets to know each learner over months and years – their strengths, their recurring weak spots, what works for them – and helps them grow. The longer you learn with it, the better it knows you. And that knowledge stays on your device.

  1. Stage 1

    Understand mistakes

    AI recognises why an answer was wrong – false friend, spelling, gender, a mix-up – and gives a one-line memory hook.

  2. Stage 2

    Knows you, coaches you

    A learner profile that grows with every round: strengths and weak spots across tests, school years and languages. The coach reshapes the plan up to test day and keeps working on what still wobbles long after it.

  3. Stage 3

    Speak it

    Guided role-plays – at the bakery, at the station, with a new classmate – built from exactly this week's words. Speech recognition stays on the phone.

What we stand for

  • Users at the centre. Every app starts with the question of what helps the people who use it. For children, that means no ads, no tracking, no dark patterns – and learning progress stays on the device.
  • The next generation of apps. AI is not a bolt-on extra for us but the core: our apps take over the tedious work, get to know their users and get better week after week.
  • A human has the final say. Agents build, a human decides: a person tests and approves every new app version before it goes to the store – and a person has checked every story and every picture in Silben Lesen before it went into the app.
  • Private by default. Whatever can be done on the device stays there. Whatever has to leave it is kept only as long as necessary and never used for advertising.
