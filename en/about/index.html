The AI-first production line

Most studios bolt AI onto an old process. We designed the process around it. Every step is an agent with a clear role, a versioned skill and a definition of done. Humans sit where judgment lives: what to build, what to kill, and what is good enough for a child.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Agents mine store data, reviews and search demand to find gaps worth filling – and kill weak ideas with numbers before a line of code exists.

  2. 02

    Concept

    A concept agent drafts the product, a critic agent tears it apart. The founder decides.

    Human decides
  3. 03

    Build

    An agent team works through sequenced work packages in parallel – often overnight, unattended, with a written brief and hard stop criteria.

  4. 04

    Create

    Claude writes the stories, a local NVIDIA GPU pipeline paints the illustrations, Amazon Polly gives them a voice.

  5. 05

    Verify

    A QA agent runs thousands of tests and audits every build. Every story, picture and voice is approved by a human.

    Human approves
  6. 06

    Ship

    Store listings, screenshots and copy come from the same line – one core, adapted to Google Play and Amazon.

AI does the work. A human owns the decisions.

AI inside the products

Ox – AI vocabulary trainer

Snap a page of any textbook and Claude on Amazon Bedrock turns it into structured flashcards in seconds – any publisher, any edition, five languages. Neural pronunciation from Amazon Polly, speaking practice with on-device speech recognition: the microphone never leaves the phone. Fully serverless on AWS Lambda.

Ox in German

Silben Lesen – learning to read

52 stories in two-colour syllable script, 149 practice rounds and 205 watercolour illustrations generated on a local NVIDIA RTX pipeline and curated by hand. Every word and every syllable is voiced – more than 6,000 clips.

Silben Lesen in German

Uhr lesen lernen – telling the time

Three learning paths and 76 rounds, with all three ways Germans say the time, spoken aloud. Live in Google Play and on Amazon Fire tablets, built and shipped in about five weeks.

In development

Next up: Ox Coach

Generic AI tutors know everything – except what is in Thursday's test. Ox already holds the vocabulary from the child's own textbook. Ox Coach turns it into a personal language coach that gets to know each learner over months and years – their strengths, their recurring weak spots, what works for them – and helps them grow. The longer you learn with it, the better it knows you. And that knowledge stays on your device.

  1. Stage 1

    Understand mistakes

    AI recognises why an answer was wrong – false friend, spelling, gender, a mix-up – and gives a one-line memory hook.

  2. Stage 2

    Knows you, coaches you

    A learner profile that grows with every round: strengths and weak spots across tests, school years and languages. The coach reshapes the plan up to test day and keeps working on what still wobbles long after it.

  3. Stage 3

    Speak it

    Guided role-plays – at the bakery, at the station, with a new classmate – built from exactly this week's words. Speech recognition stays on the phone.

Three more apps are in the pipeline, among them a licence trainer for marine radio and a family shopping list that learns. Every app makes the next one faster.
