What it does

Built for the moment in the store.

  • ✍️

    Quick to add

    “2 l milk”, “500 g mince” or a whole recipe at once – the app recognises amount and unit.

  • 🧭

    Grouped by aisle

    Fruit & vegetables, bakery, dairy and nine more, in your order. (Automatic sorting currently recognises German item names.)

  • 👥

    Shop together

    Share a list with a code. Every change shows up on every device.

  • 📶

    Works offline

    Even in the basement aisle. As soon as you have signal again, the list syncs by itself.

  • 🛒

    While shopping

    The screen stays on, checked items move down, nothing gets swiped away by accident.

  • 🔁

    Remembers what you need

    “Bought often” and the history know what goes on the list regularly.

Plus: home screen widget, voice input, adjustable text size and row height, light and dark mode.

No fuss

Your list is yours.

  • Your list only holds what you add yourselves – no product suggestions from retailers.
  • No account. Shared lists run through our own server in Frankfurt (EU); everything else stays on your device.
  • Voice input uses your device’s speech recognition – the app itself doesn’t send any recording anywhere.
  • Feedback right in the app, showing which device info is included – nothing goes along secretly.
