Shopping List

The shared shopping list – no account

For families, couples and flatmates

One of you adds “milk” at home, the other sees it instantly in the store. It's the list on the fridge – on two phones at once. No account, no sign-up, free.

What it does

  • Quick to add: “2 l milk”, “500 g mince” or a whole recipe at once – the app recognises amount and unit.
  • Grouped by aisle: fruit & vegetables, bakery, dairy and nine more. Set the aisle order once per store and walk through without zigzagging. (Automatic aisle sorting currently recognises German item names.)
  • Shop together: share a list with a code. No e-mail, no password, joined in under a minute. Every change shows up on every device.
  • Works offline: even in the basement aisle. As soon as you have signal again, the list syncs by itself.
  • While shopping: the screen stays on, checked items move down, nothing gets swiped away by accident.
  • “Bought often” and the history remember what goes on the list regularly.
  • Home screen widget, voice input, take ingredients from other apps (e.g. a recipe) via share.
  • Adjustable text size and row height, light and dark mode.

No fuss

  • Your list only holds what you add yourselves – no product suggestions from retailers.
  • No account. Shared lists run through our own server in Frankfurt (EU); everything else stays on your device.
  • Voice input uses your device's speech recognition – the app itself doesn't send any recording anywhere.
  • Feedback right in the app, showing which device info is included – nothing goes along secretly.

Free, no subscription

The app is free. The first 5 days have no ads at all, after that a small banner at the bottom – never inside your list, no full-screen ads, no videos. If you'd rather not see it, buy “No ads” once for €1.49 – it applies to your Google account, no subscription.
