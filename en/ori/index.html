Ori Vokabeltrainer

The vocabulary trainer that knows your textbook

From grade 3 · English, French, Spanish, Latin and Italian · the app is in German, for pupils at German-speaking schools

Every vocabulary trainer can quiz you. The catch is always the same: someone has to type the words in first. Ori skips the typing. Photograph the vocabulary page – an AI reads out words, meanings and example sentences in seconds –, check them briefly, done. It does not matter which textbook or edition the school uses; only what is on the page counts. The vocabulary notebook and the teacher's worksheet work too.

Practise for the test, not into the blue

A vocabulary test has a date. Enter it and you get a plan up to that day: daily rounds with the words that are still shaky, a practice test a few days before and a last check on the day of the test. After the test the words stay in the deck and come back at longer intervals.

Made for parents

  • No ads, no account, no tracking
  • Parents' area with three numbers: secure, shaky, new
  • No time pressure, no lives, no leaderboards
  • Learning progress stays on the device
  • Purchases go through Google Play – with purchase authentication or Family Link, parents decide on every purchase

What it costs

The app is free, and entering words by hand stays free without limit. Only importing from a photo is paid, because every picture costs computing time: the first page is free, after that either a one-off purchase for a thousand words or a monthly subscription that can be cancelled any time in Google Play.
