Our apps

One job per app.

All four are live on Google Play; Silben Lesen and Uhr lesen lernen are also in the Amazon Appstore.

From grade 3 · English, French, Spanish, Latin, Italian

Ubo – Vokabeltrainer

The vocabulary trainer that learns the words from your own textbook, in five languages: photograph the page, the AI reads out the vocabulary – then practise specifically for the test.

More about the app →

Primary school · reading

Silben Lesen

Learning to read with the syllable method: 58 stories and 7 books in two-colour syllable script, 149 practice rounds, over 350 watercolour pictures. For beginning readers in primary school.

More about the app →

Primary school · telling the time

Uhr lesen lernen

Telling the time for primary school: 200 practice rounds up to the 24-hour clock, colour-coded hands that fade away as a learning aid, and both German ways of saying the time.
