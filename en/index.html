Our promise

Fair to children and parents.

This holds for every one of our learning apps, today and in future.

  • Buy once, use forever. A fair price, no subscription needed.
  • No ads. Nothing distracts your child, nothing lures them elsewhere.
  • No account. No sign-up; progress stays on the device.
  • No racing against others. Your child only competes with themselves.
  • Honest with you. You can see where your child stands and what is still missing.
