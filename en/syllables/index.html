Silben Lesen

Learning to read with the syllable method

For beginning readers in primary school · the app is in German

Silben Lesen is a reading app for children who learn to read German with the syllable method – in two-colour syllable script (blue/red), just like in the school exercise book. 58 original stories and 7 books lead from the very first sentence („Mi|la ist da.") to a full picture-book page; 149 practice rounds train hearing syllables, recognising words and sliding syllables into words.

What the app offers

  • 58 stories and 7 books in two-colour syllable script, graded by difficulty
  • 149 reading exercises: hearing syllables, hearing words, syllable puzzle
  • Tap any word to hear it
  • Over 350 lovingly made watercolour illustrations
  • A calm learning path with growing flowers instead of chasing points

Made for parents

  • No ads, no subscription, no account, no tracking
  • Fully offline – the app needs no internet, except for the one purchase
  • Try 20 stories and exercises and one book for free, then a one-off purchase – no subscription
  • Parents' area showing your child's progress
