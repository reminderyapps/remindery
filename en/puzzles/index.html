Kinder Rätselheft 1. & 2. Klasse

The puzzle book that never runs out

For children in year 1 and 2 · German only – the app is in German

Kinder Rätselheft is the puzzle book from the stationery shop as an app – except it never fills up. 30 kinds of puzzles in five areas, each in three levels, and every page is freshly generated. Each puzzle is introduced by a short spoken instruction (in German) and shown once by a demo hand, so children can get going on their own.

What the app offers

  • 30 kinds of puzzles in five areas: logic (incl. picture sudoku), spot the difference and seeking, words, arithmetic, mazes and paths
  • Easy, medium and hard for every kind of puzzle
  • Endless pages – and a lucky bag that mixes seven puzzles from all areas
  • Works offline, portrait and landscape, phone and tablet

Made for parents

  • No ads, no subscription, no account, no tracking
  • 104 puzzles free, then a one-off purchase of €1.99 for everything
  • Purchases only in the parents' area
